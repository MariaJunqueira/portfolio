Concept project · 2026

Shiftline

A weekly roster where managers drag shifts between people and instantly see conflicts.

RoleDesign & frontend
TypeSelf-initiated concept
StackTypeScript, vanilla DOM

The problem

Scheduling tools get cluttered fast. Managers need to move shifts quickly, and they need to know right away when a move double-books someone or breaks a rest rule.

Try it

Click a shift, then click a day cell to move it. Moving a second shift onto the same person and day flags a conflict. Keyboard friendly.

Select a shift to begin.

Key decisions

  • Click-to-move over pure drag: works with keyboard and touch, drag can be layered on top.
  • Conflicts are non-blocking: flagged in a second colour plus text, never colour alone.
  • Live region announcements so screen-reader users get the same feedback.

What I'd do next

Undo/redo, rest-time rules per country, and optimistic sync with a backend.
