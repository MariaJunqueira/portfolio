About me

Hi, I'm Maria.

I'm a frontend engineer from Brazil. I studied computer science in Minas Gerais, built fintech products in São Paulo, moved to Porto to work on banking and mobility software, and now live in the Netherlands, where I'm a Senior Frontend Engineer at Shiftbase.

I care about the details people feel but rarely notice: a page that loads instantly, a form that's easy to fill in, a button that works with a keyboard. I like turning messy problems into clear architecture, and helping teams ship with confidence.

Outside work, I run Gryfi, where I make cozy games like Potions Purr and gentle apps like Gremlin.
